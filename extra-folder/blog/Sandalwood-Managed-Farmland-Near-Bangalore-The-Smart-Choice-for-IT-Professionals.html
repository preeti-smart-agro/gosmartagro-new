Sandalwood Managed Farmland Near Bangalore: The Smart Choice for IT Professionals

Bangalore IT professionals often feel stuck with investments that grow slowly or require constant attention. Apartments in Whitefield or Sarjapur give small rent, plots near Devanahalli take years to sell, and FDs offer minimal post-tax returns.

For busy techies, a hands-off investment with steady growth and multiple income streams is the ideal solution. 1-acre managed sandalwood farmland near Bangalore ticks all these boxes.

Why Traditional Investments Fall Short

Why Sandalwood Managed Farmland Outshines Other Investments

1. Location Advantage: NH544E & Airport Corridor

  • Devanahalli-Hindupur corridor near NH544E → 8–12% annual land appreciation.

  • Close to Bangalore International Airport (BIAL) and IT SEZ hubs → higher resale value.

  • Proximity to BEL and KIADB industrial zones → logistics advantage and safety.

2. Sandalwood Trees

  • 40–60 trees per acre.

  • Maturity: 15–20 years.

  • Heartwood price: ₹6,000–₹10,000/kg.

  • Potential per-acre value at maturity: ₹6–10 crore.

3. Intercropping Income

  • Crops like guava, groundnut, millets, vegetables.

  • Generates ₹50,000–₹1.5 lakh per year per acre.

  • Covers labour, irrigation, manure, and security costs.

4. Land Appreciation

  • 1 acre bought at ₹40 lakh today → ₹1–2 crore in 15 years.
