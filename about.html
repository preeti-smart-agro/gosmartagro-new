About Our Company

Our Company Vision & Mission Competitive Advantage

Smart Agro

SmartAgro is a leading agro-reality company headquartered in Bangalore, India, specializing in managed farmland solutions that combine sustainable agriculture with high-value timber plantations. Established with the vision of delivering profitable and eco-conscious farm investments, SmartAgro offers legally secure, fully-managed farmplots that are developed, cultivated, and maintained by experts—so investors can enjoy farm ownership without the hassle.

We are proud to be an ISO-certified organization and were recently awarded as the Best Agro-Realty Company for our innovation, integrity, and impact in the agro-investment space. With 300+ happy co-farmers already part of our journey, SmartAgro continues to grow as a trusted name in sandalwood managed farmland near Bangalore. Our in-house team brings over 30 years of combined expertise in agriculture, timber trade, horticulture, and sustainable land management, ensuring each project delivers ecological value and strong financial returns.

Vision

To establish SmartAgro as the most loved and trusted agro-realty company in India—one that builds highly profitable farming businesses and delivers a life of happiness to our employees, partners, and, most importantly, our customers.


Mission

To deliver a ‘wow’ experience to our customers every single day by building profit-making managed farm entities that reconnect people with nature, offer access to healthy and affordable farm produce, and enrich lives with true joy and fulfillment.

Founder & CEO
Mr. Kiran Kumar

Mr. Ramchandra Kiran Kumar, the founder of SmartAgro is a software engineer by profession but an environment-centric entrepreneur by choice. A critical thinker since his early days and having had experience in construction too, he always had been on the lookout for solutions to social, economic and environmental problems. Agro-realty for him, was the answer to generating profits for consumers, serving the timber demands of the nation and do all of this in a manner that benefits the environment.

Working with integrity and professionalism in everything that he takes up, Mr. Kiran’s vision is to scale up SmartAgro in the coming decade to be one of the most respected & valued agro-realty company.

Dr. Srikant Swamy K - Lead Scientist - Profitable Farming Models

Dr. Swamy brings to Smart Agro a refreshing approach to creating profitable farming models. He is an accomplished scientist with 33 years of rich experience in research & development dedicated to agricultural science and sustainable crop development

All through his illustrious career, Dr. Srikant had been associated for the larger part of it with the Central Silk Board, making it his purpose to promote sericulture, which is the cultivation of silk worms to produce silk.

Fortunately, his work exposed him to advanced soil conservation practices, management of crop disease, dry land farming techniques, eco-technology practices, usage of vermi-compost in soil conservation, organic farming and more.

Dr. Swamy has published 35 international and 55 national papers in agriculture related topics. He has been awarded a fellow membership at the International Congress on Environmental Research under the aegis of Global Earth Society for Environmental Energy & Development. The award was for his efforts to develop appropriate technology for recycling seri-waste in to bioenergy, carbon removal and sequestration for sustainable crop and environmental development.

Dr. Swamy has filed a patent for technique of converting waste mulberry shoots into biofuel. He is also presently working on the role of mulberry and its waste in carbon removal, sequestration and bioenergy recovery, all of which relate to sustainable crop and environmental development

Dr. Swamy has developed advanced technology in mulberry plantation in moisture constraint areas, affordable micro-irrigation, seri-lac dual culture model for income augmentation and briquetting.

Dr. Swamy was awarded a Certificate of Appreciation by the Japan International Co-operative Agency for the excellent efforts in implementing Biovoltine sericulture technology in India under the PEBS Project.

Dr. Swamy won the ‘Best Merit Scientist’ in 2008 for his outstanding contribution to sericulture during the year at the Central Sericultural Research and Training Institute

On multiple occasions, Dr. Swamy has been awarded certificate of appreciations for his significant contributions towards development of bio-stimulant- Dr. Soil and promoting Biovoltine sericulture s

Dr. Swamy has a M.Sc. degree in Botany (focus: cytogenetics) from Bangalore University and he has earned a Ph.D. in Applied Botany from the University of Mysore.

Dr. Srikant Swamy’s rich experience and zest to be inventive in every new challenge is a tremendous asset to Smart Agro

At SmartAgro, Dr. Swamy’s work has been cut out for him, clearly. He will provide his expertise in building an integrated farming system for sustainable income generation. This exercise would use NRM in moisture constraint areas. Dr. Swamy will also help in setting up low cost, affordable micro irrigation technologies (AMIT) at agro-realty projects by SmartAgro. Over and above all this, he will also provide solutions to the water, labor and energy problems as and when they occur.
