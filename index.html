Eco-Friendly Projects

At SmartAgro, sustainability is not a feature—it’s a foundation. Our agro-realty projects are deeply rooted in environmental responsibility, driven by a shared vision among our founders to create positive ecological impact through every acre developed. From the very beginning, SmartAgro was born out of a desire to do more than generate profit—we aim to restore green cover, protect biodiversity, and build a better future through responsible farmland development.

Our flagship projects, Timber and GreenX, are more than just investment opportunities. They are active afforestation efforts that contribute to the ecological well-being of their regions. By planting high-value timber trees like sandalwood, along with companion crops, we promote soil regeneration, local climate balance, and long-term carbon sequestration. These projects not only reduce the carbon footprint but also enhance biodiversity and restore underutilized rural land to productive, thriving ecosystems.

We also integrate environmentally friendly farming practices, including organic inputs, drip irrigation, intercropping, and tech-enabled land and water management systems. This ensures maximum yield with minimal environmental impact. Every managed farmplot—whether part of the GreenX project near Bangalore or the Timber project in Hindupur—is designed to harmonize profit with the plane

Timber - SmartAgro's First Flagship Managed Farmland Project

Timber by SmartAgro is our pioneering agro-realty project, launched with the vision of turning sustainable forestry into a profitable investment model. Located in Hindupur, in Andhra Pradesh’s Anantapur district, the project benefits from fertile soil, excellent connectivity by road, rail, and air, and its close proximity—just an hour's drive—from Bangalore. Positioned in an emerging industrial belt, TIMBER spans over 50 acres of legally verified land, divided into managed farmplots and cultivated with high-value sandalwood trees and commercial crop

SmartAgro handles everything—from land acquisition and development to plantation, irrigation, and harvest—making Timber a truly hassle-free managed farmland opportunity. The project is structured to generate short-term income from intercrops and long-term returns from sandalwood, along with steady land appreciation. TIMBER isn’t just about farmland ownership—it’s about owning your personal slice of a thriving sandalwood managed farmland near Bangalore, while building wealth through ecological, tax-free, and sustainable means

GreenX - SmartAgro's Second Signature Managed Farmland Project

GreenX is the second ongoing project by SmartAgro, offering a premium opportunity to own sandalwood managed farmland near Bangalore. Designed for urban investors seeking both sustainability and returns, GreenX features legally clear managed farmplots within a secure, gated community. Each plot comes with professionally planted sandalwood trees, supplemented by intercropping of commercial crops for short-term income. With end-to-end management, including soil care, irrigation, pest control, and harvest planning, GreenX ensures a completely hands-free and profitable experience.

As part of SmartAgro's commitment to eco-conscious agro-realty, GreenX stands out as a fully managed farmland solution that blends investment, lifestyle, and environmental stewardship. Located near high-growth corridors and SEZ zones, this project offers strong land appreciation potential alongside tax-free agricultural income. Whether you're looking to diversify your portfolio or build a long-term green legacy, GreenX delivers the ideal mix of value, convenience, and ecological impact—all just a short drive from Bangalore
